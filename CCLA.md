# Hunch Corporate Contributor Assignment Agreement

Version 1.0

An organization signs this Corporate Agreement when its employees or contractors
contribute to the Hunch project (the "Project") as part of their work for it.
Each of those individuals also signs the
[Contributor Assignment Agreement](CLA.md) ("Individual Agreement").

## 1. Terms

The organization named below ("Corporation") makes to the Owner (as defined in
the Individual Agreement, including any transferee under its Section 7) the same
assignment, fallback license, moral-rights waiver, promises and undertakings as
Sections 2 to 6 of the Individual Agreement, for every Contribution that a
Designated Contributor listed in Schedule A has submitted or later submits to the
Project. In those sections, and notwithstanding the definition in Section 2,
"You" means Corporation.

Corporation also represents that:

1. The person signing below is authorized to bind Corporation to this Corporate
   Agreement.
2. Each Designated Contributor is authorized to submit Contributions on its
   behalf.
3. Corporation owns the rights in its Designated Contributors' Contributions that
   this Corporate Agreement assigns.

Corporation adds or removes Designated Contributors by sending the Owner an
updated Schedule A signed by an authorized person. Removal does not affect
Contributions already submitted. This Corporate Agreement takes effect on the
date signed below, and covers Contributions submitted before that date.

Sections 7 (Transfer by the Owner), 8 (No warranty; no obligation) and 9
(General) of the Individual Agreement apply to this Corporate Agreement. An
electronic signature, or a signed copy sent as a PDF, binds Corporation as fully
as a signed original.
