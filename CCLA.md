# Hunch Corporate Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0

An organization signs this Corporate Contributor License Agreement ("Corporate
Agreement") when its employees or contractors contribute to the Hunch project
(the "Project") as part of their work for it. Each of those individuals also
signs the [Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) ("Individual
Agreement").

## 1. Terms

The organization named below ("Corporation") grants the Owner (as defined in the
Individual Agreement, including any assignee under its Section 8) the same
copyright license, patent license, invention assignment, moral-rights waiver and
patent undertakings, and makes the same promises, as Sections 2 to 7 of the
Individual Agreement, for every Contribution that a Designated Contributor listed
in Schedule A has submitted or later submits to the Project, and for every
invention of a Designated Contributor that Corporation owns and that Section 5
of the Individual Agreement covers. In those sections, and notwithstanding the
definition in Section 2, "You" means Corporation.

Corporation also represents that:

1. The person signing below is authorized to bind Corporation to this Corporate
   Agreement.
2. Each Designated Contributor is authorized to submit Contributions on its
   behalf.
3. Corporation owns, or has the right to license and assign as this Corporate
   Agreement requires, the intellectual property in its Designated Contributors'
   Contributions and in the inventions it covers.

Corporation adds or removes Designated Contributors by sending the Owner an
updated Schedule A signed by an authorized person. Removal does not affect
Contributions already submitted. This Corporate Agreement takes effect on the
date signed below, and covers Contributions submitted before that date.

Sections 8 (Assignment), 9 (No warranty; no obligation) and 10 (General) of the
Individual Agreement apply to this Corporate Agreement. An electronic signature,
or a signed copy sent as a PDF, binds Corporation as fully as a signed original.
